About Capital Hardware

Capital Hardware has been Sri Lanka's leading hardware supplier for over two decades, providing quality products and exceptional service to customers across the island. Established in 1999, we have built our reputation on trust, reliability, and an unwavering commitment to excellence.

Our extensive product range includes everything from basic hand tools to sophisticated machinery, construction materials, electrical supplies, and plumbing equipment. We serve both individual customers and large-scale commercial projects, ensuring that everyone receives the same high standard of service and product quality.

What sets Capital Hardware apart is our deep understanding of the local market combined with access to international quality standards. We carefully select our suppliers and products to ensure that our customers receive the best value for their investment. Our team of experienced professionals is always ready to provide expert advice and technical support.

Located in the heart of Colombo, our flagship store at Old Moor Street has become a landmark for hardware enthusiasts and professionals alike. We continue to expand our services and product offerings to meet the evolving needs of Sri Lanka's construction and industrial sectors.
